⚠️ Key Cross-Tapering & Safety Rules

  • SSRI ↔ SSRI or SNRI: Direct switch at equivalent dose is generally tolerated for low-to-moderate doses; use a 1–2 week conservative cross-taper when switching off short-half-life agents (paroxetine, venlafaxine, duloxetine) to prevent discontinuation dizziness/paresthesias.
  • Switching TO/FROM Fluoxetine: Fluoxetine/norfluoxetine persists 4–6 weeks; stop fluoxetine and wait 4–7 days before starting low-dose replacement (and allow a full 5-week washout before any MAOI).
  • Switching TO/FROM Bupropion: Bupropion lacks serotonergic activity—never abruptly stop a high-dose SSRI/SNRI when starting bupropion; taper the SSRI/SNRI gradually over 2–4 weeks while titrating bupropion.
