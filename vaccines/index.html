CDC ACIP 2024–2025 General Best Practice Pearls

• Do NOT Restart Interrupted Series: Regardless of time elapsed between doses, routine vaccine series do not need to be restarted from dose 1.
• Simultaneous Administration: Most live and non-live vaccines can be administered at the same clinic visit using different anatomical injection sites (separated by ≥1 inch).
• Live Vaccine Separation Rule: Parenteral live vaccines (MMR, Varicella, Yellow Fever) not given on the same day must be separated by at least 4 weeks (28 days).
