Clinical Reference Standards

• Baseline Anti-inflammatory Equivalence: Hydrocortisone 20 mg ≈ Prednisone 5 mg ≈ Prednisolone 5 mg ≈ Methylprednisolone 4 mg ≈ Triamcinolone 4 mg ≈ Dexamethasone 0.75 mg.
• Short bursts (≤14 days) generally do not require prolonged hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal (HPA) axis tapering unless the patient has received recent repeated systemic steroid courses.
• Taking daily doses in the morning (QAM) reduces circadian HPA-axis suppression and insomnia.
