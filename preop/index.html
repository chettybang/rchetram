ASA & ACC/AHA Perioperative Consensus Pearls

• GLP-1 Aspiration Warning (ASA 2023): Hold weekly GLP-1/GIP agonists for 7 days prior to elective procedures to prevent delayed gastric emptying and aspiration under anesthesia.
• SGLT2 Euglycemic DKA Alert (FDA): Hold Empagliflozin/Dapagliflozin/Canagliflozin 3 days (Ertugliflozin 4 days) prior to surgery.
• Never Stop Beta-Blockers: Abrupt cessation of chronic beta-blockers increases perioperative ischemia and mortality. Continue on morning of surgery with a small sip of water.
