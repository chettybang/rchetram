Clinical Practice Pearls

• Basal Titration Rule: Increase basal dose by 2 units every 3 days until fasting blood glucose target (80–130 mg/dL) is met without hypoglycemia.
• NPH to Glargine Conversion: Always reduce the total daily NPH dose by 20% when converting NPH BID to Glargine QD to prevent nocturnal hypoglycemia.
• Correction Factor (1800 Rule): Sensitivity Factor = 1800 / TDD. Tells how many mg/dL 1 unit of rapid-acting insulin analog lowers blood glucose.
