Quick Point-of-Care Safety Checklist

GLP-1 / GIP Agents: Contraindicated in MTC, MEN 2, Pregnancy/Lactation, or history of severe acute pancreatitis.
Qsymia (Phentermine/Topiramate): Contraindicated in Pregnancy (REMS cleft palate risk), Glaucoma, Hyperthyroidism, MAOIs. Requires gradual taper (seizure risk).
Contrave (Bupropion/Naltrexone): Contraindicated in Seizures, Chronic Opioids (precipitates withdrawal), Eating Disorders (Bulimia/Anorexia), Abrupt ETOH detox.
Phentermine Monotherapy: Avoid in Uncontrolled HTN (SBP >160 or DBP >100), Severe CVD, Glaucoma, Agitation, or MAOI use within 14 days.
