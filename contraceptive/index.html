⏱️ CDC 2024 U.S. SPR Missed-Pill Rules

Combined Oral Contraceptives (COCs)

  • 1 Active Pill Missed (<48h since last pill / <24h late): Take missed pill ASAP, continue 1 pill daily; no backup contraception needed.
  • ≥2 Consecutive Active Pills Missed (≥48h since a pill was due): Take most recent missed pill ASAP, discard earlier missed pills, continue 1 pill daily, and use backup protection for 7 days. If missed in Week 1 with unprotected sex in past 5 days, consider EC (if ulipristal/Ella is used, wait 5 days before restarting COC). If missed in Week 3, finish active pills and skip the placebo week.

Progestin-Only Pills (POPs) — Window Differs by Progestin!

  • Norethindrone 0.35 mg & Norgestrel 0.075 mg (Opill): Strict 3-hour window. If taken >3 hours late, take 1 pill ASAP, continue daily, and use backup protection for 2 days (48h).
  • Drospirenone 4 mg (Slynd — 24/4 Pack): Forgiving 24-hour window. If 1 active pill is <24 hours late (<48h since prior pill), take ASAP—no backup needed. If ≥2 consecutive active pills missed (≥48h since scheduled), take most recent pill ASAP and use backup protection for 7 days.
