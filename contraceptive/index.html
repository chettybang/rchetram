CDC Selected Practice Recommendations (SPR) Summary

• 7-Day Rule for Combined OCPs: If 2 or more active combined pills are missed, backup contraception (condoms) or abstinence is required until active pills have been taken for 7 consecutive days.
• 3-Hour Window for Traditional POPs: Traditional Norethindrone POPs must be taken within a strict 3-hour window daily. If >3 hours late, backup contraception is needed for 48 hours.
• Emergency Contraception (EC): Plan B (Levonorgestrel 1.5mg) is most effective within 72 hours; Ella (Ulipristal acetate 30mg) or Copper IUD is preferred if BMI > 30 kg/m² or up to 120 hours post-intercourse.
