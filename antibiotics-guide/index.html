Safety, Allergy & Renal Profile

IDSA & AAP Antimicrobial Stewardship Standards

• Outpatient Short-Course Principles: Guidelines prioritize shorter duration courses (e.g., 3 days for cystitis, 5 days for CAP/cellulitis) to reduce C. difficile and resistance rates. • Inpatient De-Escalation: Re-evaluate empirical inpatient broad-spectrum coverage (MRSA/Pseudomonas) at 48–72 hours based on blood/sputum/urine cultures and rapid MRSA PCR nasal swabs. • Pediatric Dosing Safeguard: All pediatric liquid suspensions are capped at maximum adult single dosages.
