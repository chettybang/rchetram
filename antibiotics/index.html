IDSA Stewardship & Safety Principles

• Shorter Courses are Safer: IDSA guidelines emphasize shorter evidence-based courses (e.g., 3 days for cystitis, 5 days for CAP, 5 days for cellulitis) to minimize C. difficile infection and resistance.
• Cephalosporin Cross-Reactivity: True cross-reactivity between Penicillins and 3rd/4th generation Cephalosporins (e.g., Cefdinir, Ceftriaxone) is <1–2%. Cephalosporins are generally safe in non-severe rash histories.
• Fluoroquinolone Black Box Warning: Avoid Ciprofloxacin or Levofloxacin for uncomplicated cystitis, sinusitis, or acute bronchitis unless no alternative exists due to risks of tendinitis, neuropathy, aortic dissection, and QTc prolongation.
