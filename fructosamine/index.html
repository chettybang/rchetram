🧬 Fructosamine to Estimated A1C

Converts serum fructosamine (µmol/L) into estimated HbA1c (%, DCCT) reflecting 2–3 weeks of average glycemia.

Estimated A1C output
-- % DCCT
Effective Fructosamine: -- µmol/L
Est. Avg Glucose (eAG): -- mg/dL

Formula: HbA1c (%) = (0.017 × Fructosamine) + 1.61 (Cohen et al.). Useful when HbA1c is unreliable (hemoglobinopathies, hemolytic anemia, CKD/ESRD, or recent transfusion). Normal fructosamine reference range: ~200–285 µmol/L.
