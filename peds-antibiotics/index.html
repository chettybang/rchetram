High-Yield Pediatric Prescribing Pearls

• High-Dose Amoxicillin (80–90 mg/kg/day): Recommended for AOM to overcome penicillin-nonsusceptible S. pneumoniae.
• Augmentin Ratio Selection: Use Augmentin ES-600 (600mg amox / 42.9mg clavulanate per 5mL) for high-dose regimens to minimize clavulanate-induced diarrhea.
